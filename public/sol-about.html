Our Story

Sugar Oak Lane is our family-owned and operated cut-flower farm in Loganville, Georgia — rooted in a love of agriculture, homesteading, flowers, and the joy of growing.

Welcome

We are the Hemingways.

Established in 2018, Sugar Oak Lane is our family-owned and operated cut-flower farm in Loganville, Georgia. Jonathan and Nakita are both fifth-generation farmers with a shared love of agriculture, homesteading, and everything that comes with building a life around the land.

Sugar Oak Lane brings that heritage forward through seasonal flowers, heirloom seeds, dahlia tubers, plants, farm goods, floral design, and experiences that make flowers and gardening feel welcoming to everyday customers and beginning gardeners.

"We hope you enjoy our seasonal offerings of blooms, heirloom seeds, farm goods and more."

— Nakita Hemingway

Sugar Oak Lane Today

Rooted in farming. Growing forward.

From our home base in Loganville, Georgia, Sugar Oak Lane continues to grow beyond a traditional flower farm. The farm, floral shop, garden offerings, weddings, workshops, and wholesale relationships all belong to one idea: helping more people enjoy flowers and feel confident growing them.

Our public-facing focus is the everyday customer and beginning gardener. That means beautiful products, clear information, practical guidance, and a shopping experience that feels inspiring without requiring professional growing knowledge.
