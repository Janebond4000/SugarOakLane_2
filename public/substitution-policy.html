Our Commitment to You

Substitution Policy

What happens when a flower isn't available — and what you can do about it.

Sugar Oak Lane grows with the seasons. That means sometimes a specific flower in your arrangement isn't at its peak on the day we cut for you. When that happens, we substitute thoughtfully — never downgrading quality, always maintaining the spirit of your order.

When We Substitute

Substitutions happen when: a specific bloom isn't available due to weather or crop timing; a flower isn't at peak quality on your order date; supply of a specific variety runs out. We substitute with flowers of equivalent or greater value that maintain the overall design integrity.

How We Choose Substitutions

  • Color-first: We match the color palette before anything else
  • Stem-for-stem value: Substitutes are never of lesser value than the original
  • Style integrity: We maintain the overall feel (garden-style stays garden-style, lush stays lush)
  • Size matters: We won't substitute a 3-inch dahlia with a tiny button flower

Specifying Must-Have Flowers

You can specify up to 2 flowers that are non-negotiable for your order. Write them clearly in your order notes with the label "MUST HAVE" and we'll make every effort to honor them or contact you before substituting.

Example: "MUST HAVE: Café au Lait dahlias. MUST HAVE: Sweet peas." We'll work around those two and substitute freely elsewhere if needed.

Common Substitution Examples

If We Can't Provide We Might Substitute
Café au Lait dahlia Similar blush/peach dinnerplate variety
Sweet peas Lisianthus or ranunculus in same palette
Sunflowers Rudbeckia or tithonia in similar warm tones
Eucalyptus Seeded eucalyptus, Italian ruscus, or similar foliage

Questions?

If you'd like to discuss your order's flowers in detail before we cut, reach out at hello@sugaroaklane.com at least 48 hours before your order date. We're happy to talk through what's growing and what's at peak.
