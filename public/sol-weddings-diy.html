DIY Wedding Flowers

Seasonal flower buckets and bulk stems for couples who want to create their own arrangements. Tell us your date, palette, and needs and we’ll confirm what is available.

Request a DIY Bucket Quote →

Fresh stems. Your creativity.

Our DIY option is for couples who want to do the arranging themselves. Tell us your date, palette, approximate quantities, and the pieces you plan to make. We’ll review seasonal availability and provide a quote based on the flowers and quantities that make sense for your event.

1
You choose your palette
Tell us your color story, preferred flowers, and any must-haves. We'll confirm what's in season and build a custom stem list just for your wedding.
2
We prepare your flowers
We harvest everything the morning of pickup. Stems go directly into water buckets. No cold storage, maximum freshness — flowers the way they're meant to be.
3
You arrange
Pick up your flowers from the farm (or arrange for local delivery). We include a care sheet and basic arranging tips. Our workshops can also prep you ahead of time.
