For the Hands-On Bride

DIY Wedding Flowers

Buckets of farm-fresh stems, ready for you to arrange. Get professional-quality flowers at a fraction of the cost — straight from our fields to your hands.

Request a DIY Bucket Quote →

Fresh stems. Your creativity.

Our DIY program is simple: you tell us what you need, we grow and cut it, and you pick it up from the farm the morning of (or the day before) your wedding. You get the same quality stems we'd use in our own arrangements — dahlias, zinnias, sweet peas, eucalyptus, lisianthus, whatever's at peak — at bulk stem prices, not arranged-bouquet prices. It's perfect for brides who have craft skills, a creative bridal party, or family members who want to contribute.

1
You choose your palette
Tell us your color story, preferred flowers, and any must-haves. We'll confirm what's in season and build a custom stem list just for your wedding.
2
We grow and cut
We harvest everything the morning of pickup. Stems go directly into water buckets. No cold storage, maximum freshness — flowers the way they're meant to be.
3
You arrange
Pick up your flowers from the farm (or arrange for local delivery). We include a care sheet and basic arranging tips. Our workshops can also prep you ahead of time.
Transparent Pricing

What to Expect

Bundle Contents Approx. Price
Bridal Bucket 100–150 mixed stems, 3–5 varieties, greenery included. Perfect for bridal bouquet, bridesmaid bouquets, and a few bouts. $200 – $350
Full Wedding Bucket Set 500–700 stems, 6–10 varieties, full greenery package. Enough to cover bridal party, ceremony, and table centerpieces. $750 – $1,200
Custom You specify exactly what you need — variety, count, color range. We build a custom harvest list and provide a quote. Quoted individually

Pricing varies by season and variety. Spring (sweet peas, ranunculus) and fall (dahlias) are our peak quality months — and some of our most beautiful palettes.

Want to practice first?

We offer floral arranging workshops throughout the year. Booking a private workshop before your wedding is the perfect way to develop your skills, test your color palette, and feel confident on the day. Come get your hands in some flowers before the big one.
