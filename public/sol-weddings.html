Why Choose a Farm Florist

Your flowers grew here.

Working with a flower farm florist is different. We're not sourcing from a broker. We're not picking from a catalog. We grow dozens of varieties specifically for weddings — lisianthus, ranunculus, sweet peas, dahlias, anemones, and more — and we design around what's at absolute peak quality on your wedding day. That's not something a traditional florist can offer. They're constrained by what's available through distribution. We're constrained only by the seasons — which means if your wedding is in July, you're getting dahlias that came out of the ground that morning.

We work with a limited number of weddings per year. Not because we want to seem exclusive — but because we want every wedding to get our full attention. If you're interested, reach out early. Our spring and fall dates fill first.

Full Service
Bridal bouquets, bridesmaid bouquets, boutonnieres, centerpieces, ceremony arch, reception installations — full floral design service.
Farm-Grown Everything
We grow what we sell. Every stem comes from our fields. No imported flowers, no cold storage fillers.
