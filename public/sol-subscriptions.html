Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I gift a subscription?

Yes! During checkout, select "This is a gift" and include a personalized card message. We'll deliver directly to your recipient with a handwritten note from you.

What flowers are included?

Arrangements feature seasonal, farm-grown blooms that change throughout the growing season. Think dahlias, zinnias, lisianthus, sunflowers, and more — every delivery is unique and designed to reflect what's at its peak on the farm.

Can I pause or cancel?

Absolutely. You can pause or cancel your subscription at any time — just contact us at hello@sugaroaklane.com and we'll take care of it. We ask for 48 hours notice before your next scheduled delivery.

What days do you deliver?

Subscription deliveries are available on Tuesdays and Thursdays within our delivery area (Atlanta metro & Loganville, GA). When you sign up, you'll choose your preferred delivery day.
