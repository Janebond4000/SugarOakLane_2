Farm-Direct

Farm-direct. No middleman.

When you order wholesale from Sugar Oak Lane, you're buying directly from the grower. No broker, no warehouse, no cross-country cold chain. Your flowers came out of the ground this week. For florists who care about quality and provenance — and for restaurants and event planners who want a genuine farm story to share with clients — that matters.

We sell to local florists, event designers, restaurants doing table arrangements, wedding planners, and corporate event coordinators. We also sell retail — everyone is welcome. We're selective about our wholesale relationships because we have limited production — we'd rather have a few great long-term customers than try to supply everyone.

Seasonal Production

What We Grow for Wholesale

Our growing season runs spring through fall with year-round dried botanicals. Availability changes weekly — we send a standing list to active wholesale customers every Monday morning.

March – May
Spring
  • Sweet peas (fragrant heirloom mix)
  • Ranunculus — peach, rose, white, coral
  • Anemones — 'Meron' series
  • Lisianthus — single and double
  • Tulips — specialty varieties
  • Snapdragons — full-stem cutting types
  • Stock — heavily fragrant, assorted
  • Larkspur — lavender, white, pink
June – October
Summer + Fall
  • Dahlias — 50+ varieties, all sizes
  • Zinnias — 'Benary's Giant' + specialty
  • Sunflowers — single-stem, branching
  • Cosmos — double and single-form
  • Celosia — cockscomb and plume types
  • Globe amaranth — dried or fresh
  • Strawflowers — air-dry beautifully
  • Gomphrena — all season, long-lasting
Year-Round (where available)
Foliage + Dried
  • Eucalyptus — seeded and preserved
  • Dried botanicals — seasonal selection
  • Foliage — seasonal greens, ferns
  • Grasses — dried ornamental grasses
  • Bunny tail grass — fresh and dried
  • Lunaria (money plant) — dried pods
