Hands-On Floral Experiences

Floral Workshops

Intimate, educational, and genuinely fun. Think petals-and-sips, but for flowers.

Seasonal, guided workshops where you create something beautiful to take home — inspired by what's growing in our fields.

View Upcoming Workshops

Flowers, hands, good company.

Our workshops happen offsite — we bring the farm to you at a venue near you. That's part of what makes it feel different from a typical floral design class in a studio. We show up with buckets of freshly cut stems straight from our fields, and walk you through the arranging process.

Each guest works with a selection of seasonal farm flowers and builds an arrangement to take home.

3 hours
All skill levels
