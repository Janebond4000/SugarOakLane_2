Seeds + Bulbs

The exact varieties from our fields, packaged for your garden. Heirloom seeds, dahlia tubers, and specialty bulb collections — all tested in Georgia soil.

From Our Fields to Yours

We plant it. We grow it. We sell it.

Every seed packet and tuber in our shop comes from varieties we actually grow on the farm. We don't resell commodity seeds from bulk distributors. When you buy dahlia tubers from us, you're getting the same stock we plant in our own rows.

We've trialed dozens of varieties over the years and kept only the best performers for Georgia's Zone 7b/8a climate — heat-tolerant, productive, and worth the growing space. Our blog has detailed growing guides for most of what we sell.

"A cutting garden isn't about growing every flower that exists. It's about growing the right flowers incredibly well."
