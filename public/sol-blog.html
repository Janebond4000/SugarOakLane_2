Zone 7b Planting Guide
Jan
Order seeds & tubers. Plan your beds.
Feb
Direct sow larkspur & poppies. Start snapdragons indoors.
Mar
Plant ranunculus & anemone corms. Start zinnia seeds inside.
Apr ✦
Transplant sweet peas. Start dahlia tubers in pots.
May
Plant dahlias after last frost (~May 1). Direct sow zinnias.
Jun
Stake dahlias. Succession sow sunflowers every 2 weeks.
Jul–Sep
Peak cutting season. Harvest daily to encourage blooms.
Oct
Dig dahlia tubers after frost. Plant spring bulbs.
Nov–Dec
Rest, reflect, and plan next year's garden.
