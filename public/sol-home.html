Nursery & Garden

Grow Your Own Cutting Garden

Our on-farm nursery is stocked with garden-ready hydrangeas, lilacs, shrubs, and perennials — plus plug starts of our most popular cutting garden varieties. Everything is grown from seed or propagated here on the farm.

Whether you're building a backyard cutting garden or filling a commercial bed, our plants arrive healthy, rooted, and ready to thrive in Georgia's growing conditions.

  • Hydrangeas, lilacs, and flowering shrubs
  • Dahlia, zinnia, and annual cutting plug starts
  • Perennial groundcovers and garden fillers
  • All varieties field-tested on our farm
