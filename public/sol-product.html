Farm Pickup or Local Delivery

All cut flowers from Sugar Oak Lane are harvested the morning of your order and conditioned before pickup or delivery. To maximize vase life:

  • Re-cut stems at a 45° angle immediately before placing in water
  • Use clean water with flower food (included with your order)
  • Change water every 2–3 days and re-cut stems each time
  • Keep arrangements away from direct sunlight, fruit, and heat vents
