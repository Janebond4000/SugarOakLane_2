Beyond Weddings

Banquets + Special Occasions

Corporate events, fundraisers, dinner parties, galas, showers, and celebrations of all kinds. Farm-fresh flowers for every occasion.

Every gathering deserves flowers.

We've done flowers for weddings, yes. But also birthday galas, corporate dinners, fundraising events, bridal showers, baby showers, holiday parties, and retirement celebrations. The common thread: clients who want flowers that feel genuine, not generic. Farm-grown flowers carry a story. They look different from mass-market arrangements — more textured, more seasonal, more alive. And when guests ask where the flowers came from, there's an actual answer.

We work with event planners, venue coordinators, and individuals planning private celebrations. We can do everything from a single statement table arrangement to complete floral decor for a 300-person gala. Tell us what you're envisioning and we'll tell you honestly what we can do.

What We Do for Events

Corporate Events
Conference centerpieces, reception arrangements, and branded color palettes for product launches and brand events. Invoiced to business. We can coordinate directly with your event team.
Social Celebrations
Birthday parties, bridal showers, baby showers, anniversary dinners. Flowers that feel personal and intentional — not hotel-lobby generic. We'll work around your color palette and theme.
Galas + Fundraisers
Large-scale statement florals for fundraising events, charity galas, and formal dinners. We can work directly with venue and event planner teams to coordinate delivery and setup.
