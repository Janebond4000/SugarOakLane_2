Licensed Nursery

Plant Nursery

Build beautiful beds and borders with plants grown right here. We hold a Georgia nursery license and grow everything from perennials to fruiting shrubs, native plants, and hydrangeas.

From Our Ground to Yours

Beautiful beds start with the right plants.

Sugar Oak Lane holds a Georgia nursery license, which means we grow plants — not just flowers. Our nursery section was built for gardeners who want to create something lasting: pollinator gardens, cutting beds that come back every year, kitchen gardens with fruiting plants, and borders that reward you with blooms season after season.

We grow our own stock from seeds and cuttings, select varieties for zone 7b/8a performance, and sell only plants we'd plant in our own garden. Every variety has been trialed through Georgia summers — which means it's been through real heat, real humidity, and the occasional stretch of drought. If it's in our nursery, it earned its place.

"We don't just grow flowers. We grow gardens."

Licensed to Grow

Our Georgia nursery license means we meet state standards for plant health and labeling. When you buy from us, you know exactly what you're getting — no mystery plants, no mislabeled varieties. Every plant we sell is grown here, inspected, and labeled with the variety name and care requirements before it leaves the farm.
